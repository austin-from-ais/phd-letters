What I am applying to do

The question I care about is how knowledge gets from the institutions that hold it to the people who need it: how schools, hospitals and cities teach, and what simulation adds as a medium for that teaching.

For the past several years I have built XR and AI learning systems with university and clinical partners. These include mixed-reality microelectronics training with the Florida Semiconductor Institute under the US Army's SCALE program, VR vocational and emotional skill-building tools for autistic adults deployed across two Massachusetts health networks, and augmented reality for the City of Bartow and Pinellas County parks.

A review I co-authored in 2025 found that XR learning environments record almost nothing about the learner, and that the AI inside them has no access to what the learner is doing. That is the work I want to do in a PhD: build a model of the learner from the sensor data a headset already captures, have a language model reason over it about what would help this person with this task, and keep it open to inspection by instructors.

Why a PhD, and why now

The practice produced the question and cannot answer it. A deployment cannot isolate causes or be checked by a field. I want the methods to run real experiments, to have the work tested by others, and to learn under an advisor. In the long run I want a lab where this work is formalized and passed on.

What committees look for in a letter

  • Specific episodes carry more weight than general praise: something you saw me do, and what it showed you.
  • A comparison helps. Where do I stand among the students, instructors or collaborators you have worked with? Stanford's form asks this directly.
  • Stanford's prompt is a fair checklist for every school: potential for advanced study, intellectual independence, analytical thinking, clear expression, and teaching potential.
  • My record is mostly collaborative and applied, so anything you can say from direct experience about my capacity for independent research, and about why doctoral study is the right next step, answers what a committee is most likely to wonder.
  • No school states a length. One to two pages is normal.
